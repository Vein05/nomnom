Recipe: Grandma's Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs
- 2 cups chocolate chips

Bake at 375°F for 9-11 minutes. Makes about 4 dozen cookies.
